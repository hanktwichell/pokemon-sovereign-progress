Legendary Locations

The places where the 46 static legendaries can stand. In each picture the yellow ring is the spot and the player stands two squares away. Choose a Pokémon for a place from its menu, or pick one in the list and press Set here on a card. Remove takes a place you don't want off the list (Show: Removed brings it back).
