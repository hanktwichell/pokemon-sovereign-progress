Postgame · Canalave Library 2F

The Chronicles

After Sinnoh’s Hall of Fame, a Legend Archivist appears on the second floor of Canalave Library. He keeps five old tomes, the Chronicles of Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea. Each tome tells of its region’s legends, and as a tome opens, those legends turn up across Sinnoh, Johto and Kanto. The tomes open one at a time, in that order, and each opens only after you have met the last one’s key legends.

How a Chronicle opens

  1. Finish the story. The Archivist arrives with Sinnoh’s Hall of Fame, the end of the whole game.
  2. Meet Arceus. Until then his tomes won’t open for him. Arceus waits at the Sinjoh Ruins: Cynthia takes you there from Goldenrod once you carry all 16 Plates and have met Mew, Celebi, Deoxys, Jirachi, Darkrai and Shaymin.
  3. Talk to the Archivist. Your first talk after meeting Arceus opens the Unova Chronicle. From then on, each talk checks whether the next tome can open, opens it if it can, and then offers a menu of the open Chronicles.
  4. Read a topic. Each Chronicle has a few topics. Reading one tells you where its legends are and, for some, hands over the item they need or lets roaming legends loose. Roamers only start roaming once you read their topic.

“Met” means caught or beaten

Wherever one legend waits on another, it waits on that encounter being done: you caught it, or you knocked it out. Running away doesn’t count. This is your call of Nov 21: “defeating arceus is good enough.” The game keeps a record of each such legend as its battle ends (a catch made before this rule counts too).

  • A one-time legend you knocked out comes back on your next visit, so you can still catch it later.
  • A roamer you knocked out is let loose again by the Archivist after your next Hall of Fame.
  • A few things still need the Pokémon itself, because you use it: the Galar Chronicle and Necrozma (Solgaleo or Lunala, which only come from evolving Cosmog), the second Cosmog, the DNA Splicers (Kyurem caught), the Zygarde Cube, the Scrolls (Kubfu caught), the N-Solarizer and N-Lunarizer (Necrozma caught), and the two gifts in every Pokémon Center: the cowgirl’s second Calyrex (Glastrier and Spectrier caught) and the Black Belt’s second Kubfu (Kubfu caught).
  • Steven’s Red and Blue Orbs in Oreburgh (for Groudon and Kyogre) keep his rule: Latias and Latios, then Regirock, Regice and Registeel, shown in your party.

The whole chain

Each band is a Chronicle: everything in it can be met once that tome is open. Arrows show what else a legend waits on. A thick arrow into a Chronicle is the encounter that opens it, followed by a talk with the Archivist. Scroll sideways on a narrow screen.

Related, but outside the Chronicles

  • Terapagos waits at Sendoff Spring with no Chronicle: Rock Climb is all it needs.
  • Regigigas (Snowpoint Temple, after the Hall of Fame) and its Johto tablet wake once Regirock, Regice and Registeel are met. Those three also open Regieleki and Regidrago in the Galar Chronicle.
  • Arceus itself is the gate to all five tomes, and to Xerneas, Yveltal and Zygarde at the Sinjoh Ruins’ stage.

The story behind it

The legends aren’t wandering in by chance. As each tome opens, the Archivist works a little more of it out: someone opened the way for these legends on purpose, and the crown stamped on the Galar tome points to the Sovereign.
